## Processing and privacy

- Rendered locally with HyperFrames 0.8.7 at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, SDR, muted. The 2026-08-22 final-preview export contains only each recorded interface: former chapter labels, badges, explanatory overlays, end cards, and disclaimer screens are not present in the encoded video.
- Website derivatives are H.264, 720 × 1280, 30 fps, `yuv420p`, muted, and fast-start enabled.
- Only the snack-order scene contains private delivery details. Its address area is covered continuously with opaque solid masks from 27.221152–34.255152 seconds and 34.255152–35.540000 seconds. The masks were inspected again after web encoding at 27.3, 28.8, 30.6, 32.5, 34.4, and 35.3 seconds.
- The real-estate and ChatGPT scenes retain the real recorded interfaces. Their visible wording remains a one-time model-output sample, not a client case, endorsement, ranking guarantee, or transaction fact.
